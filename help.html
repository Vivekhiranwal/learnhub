Getting Started

Welcome to LearnHub! Here's how to get started with your learning journey:

  • Create your account or sign in
  • Browse our course catalog
  • Enroll in courses that interest you
  • Access your learning dashboard
  • Start learning at your own pace

Need help creating an account? Visit our Account & Profile section for detailed instructions.

Account & Profile

Creating an Account

To create a LearnHub account:

  • Click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner
  • Enter your email address and create a password
  • Complete your profile information
  • Verify your email address

Managing Your Profile

You can update your profile information at any time:

  • Click on your profile picture in the top right
  • Select "Edit Profile"
  • Update your information
  • Save your changes

Courses & Learning

Enrolling in Courses

To enroll in a course:

  • Browse our course catalog
  • Click on a course to view details
  • Click "Enroll Now"
  • Complete the enrollment process

Accessing Course Content

Once enrolled, you can access your courses:

  • Go to your dashboard
  • Click on "My Courses"
  • Select the course you want to access
  • Start learning!

Billing & Payments

Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
  • PayPal
  • Bank Transfer

Managing Subscriptions

To manage your subscription:

  • Go to your account settings
  • Select "Billing & Payments"
  • View and manage your subscription
  • Update payment methods

Technical Support

System Requirements

To use LearnHub, you need:

  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • Stable internet connection
  • Updated operating system
  • JavaScript enabled

Troubleshooting

Common issues and solutions:

  • Clear your browser cache
  • Update your browser
  • Check your internet connection
  • Try a different browser

Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions.

Q: Can I download course materials?
A: Yes, most courses allow you to download materials for offline use.

Q: How do I get a certificate?
A: Complete all course requirements and pass the final assessment.
